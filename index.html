Install on iPhone or iPad

Safari on iOS allows installing Alcove as a standalone app directly to your home screen:

1
Tap the Share button Tap the Share icon in Safari's bottom toolbar.
2
Select "Add to Home Screen" Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen .
3
Launch Standalone Tap the new Alcove icon on your home screen to enjoy fullscreen standalone writing with offline persistence.
